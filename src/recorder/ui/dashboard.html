What is recorded

The recorder watches how work moves between applications, not what you write.

Captured

  • Which application and window is in front (e.g. Excel — AP tracker.xlsx)
  • Clicks, scrolls and counts of keystrokes
  • Shortcut combinations (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+S, Alt+Tab)
  • A low-resolution screenshot when you switch windows, when the screen changes, and every 15 s
  • Copied text after redaction, and which app a paste came from, so re-keying between systems is visible

Never captured

  • Passwords or anything typed into a private app (1Password, banking, …)
  • The letters you type — only how many, unless your company turns that on
  • Emails, phone numbers, card and account numbers — replaced with [EMAIL], [PHONE], [CARD] before saving
  • Anything while the recorder is paused or off
  • Audio, webcam, or files on your disk

Where it goes

Recordings are saved to ~/Vista/recordings. Each folder holds events.jsonl (one line per interaction), shots/, screen.webm and manifest.json. Your name is replaced with a code before analysis.

Settings

Defaults are set by your company; you can always make recording stricter.

Cloud workspace

Connect to review completed reports on the website. Uploads include activity labels, app names, case IDs, notes and pseudonymous user IDs. Screenshots, video and raw interaction logs stay on this computer.

Private apps and words

Capture is muted while any of these apps are in front or these words appear in the window title.

AI explanations (OpenAI)

After a session, the AI explains each stretch of work for you to approve or fix; where it is under 88% sure it asks you to explain instead. Uses your company's OpenAI key; only window names, counts and your notes are sent — never keystrokes. OPENAI_API_KEY in the environment overrides this.
