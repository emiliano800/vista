The recorder

One Start button, on-device analysis, and an employee review step before anything is shared with your company workspace.

How a session works

  1. Start. A small always-on-top pill shows recording is active. Work as usual.
  2. Stop. The recorder analyses the session on your computer: activities, cases, timings, and automation candidates.
  3. Review. The session is split into stretches. An AI proposes an explanation for each; you approve, fix, or explain it in your own words. Unclear stretches always wait for you.
  4. Upload. Only the completed report is shared: the summary, the manifest, and the cleaned activity log.

What stays on your device

  • Screenshots and screen video never leave the computer.
  • Raw keystrokes and interaction logs never leave the computer.
  • Text is redacted on-device before analysis; reports carry activity labels, app names, case IDs and notes — not screen contents.
  • You see everything in a report before it uploads, and uploading is always a button you press, never automatic.

Connect to your company workspace

  1. Install the Vista desktop app (your administrator provides it).
  2. In the app, open Settings → Cloud workspace and enter this website address, your company ID, and your personal access key.
  3. Find your company ID in the company workspace under “Share a completed session”, and get your personal key from your workspace administrator.
  4. After a session is reviewed, choose Upload report in My recordings. Uploaded reports appear in the workspace for your company.
Keys are personal: they identify who uploaded a report. Rotating a key signs that person out everywhere.
