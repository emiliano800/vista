Desktop app

Press record. Understand the work.

The Vista recorder captures how work actually happens on your computer, uploads only activity metadata you approve, and lets you review and answer the analysis before anything is published to your company workspace.

  • One Start button and a small overlay — then work as usual.
  • Screenshots, video, window titles and keystrokes stay on the device. Only app names, timing and counts are uploaded, plus documents you tick.
  • Your workspace analyses the upload into a draft only you can see. You answer its questions and decide whether to publish.

Set up in two minutes

  1. Download and open. Drag the app into Applications. The macOS build is not yet notarised, so the first launch is blocked: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the notice about Vista Recorder and choose Open Anyway. If macOS says the app is "damaged", run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Vista Recorder.app" in Terminal once, then open it.
  2. Connect your company, once. The first launch asks for the personal access key from your administrator, your company, and your consent to what is shared. There are no other settings.
  3. Record. Press Start, work normally, press Stop.
  4. Upload, answer, publish. Approve the sharing package, wait for the draft, answer its questions, then publish to your company workspace.
Published reports appear under Recordings for your company at bumpsolutions.org/account. Every upload is verified and traceable — nothing uploads or publishes silently.
