Desktop app

Press record. Understand the work.

The Vista recorder captures how work actually happens on your computer, analyses it on the device, and lets you review every explanation before anything is shared with your company workspace.

  • One Start button and a small overlay — then work as usual.
  • Analysis runs on your computer. Screenshots, video and keystrokes stay on the device.
  • You review and approve every AI explanation; only the cleaned report is submitted, and only when you press Submit.

Free download · macOS 13+ and Windows 10+ · Your workspace administrator provides your access key.

Set up in two minutes

  1. Download and open. On macOS the app is not yet notarised: right-click the app and choose Open the first time.
  2. Connect your workspace. Open Settings → Cloud workspace and paste the personal access key from your administrator.
  3. Record. Press Start, work normally, press Stop. The session is analysed on your computer.
  4. Review and submit. Approve or fix each explanation, then Submit the report to your company workspace.
Reports appear for your company at bumpsolutions.org/account. Every submitted report is idempotent and traceable — nothing uploads silently.
