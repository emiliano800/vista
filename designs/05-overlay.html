Two weeks of normal work. One honest map.

No ERP integration, no consultants with sticky notes. A small desktop app, an always-visible pill so nobody forgets it's on, and an engine that runs on the laptop the moment you press Stop.

  • RecordStart once. The overlay pill stays on top of Excel, Outlook, anything. Private apps and keywords mute capture.
  • Redact on deviceEmails, phones, IBANs and cards are scrubbed before anything is written. Typed text is never stored.
  • Link copy to pasteA paste into QuickBooks knows it came from Acrobat 11 seconds earlier. Re-keying becomes a number.
  • Get the reportProcess map, rework loops, data flows and a ranked list of what to automate, generated locally on Stop.

Built to be run on someone else's laptop.

Employees can see it's on, can pause it, can tell it what happened off-screen — and their word outranks our rules. Nothing leaves the machine but a summary and the activity log.

  • Screenshots and video never leave the device.
  • Users are pseudonymised with a salted hash before storage.
  • Every step says where its label came from: observed, rule, filled or human.
  • Annotations split and relabel our steps. Human case IDs are never overwritten.
