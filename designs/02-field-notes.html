A clearer picture of everyday work.

Vista watches how people at a company actually do their jobs — across Excel, Outlook, the ERP, the phone — and turns it into an honest process map with the evidence attached.

Talk to usHow it works

Process maps are usually drawn from memory. Ours are drawn from Tuesdays.

When a fund buys a company, someone interviews the controller, sketches the order-to-cash flow on a whiteboard, and estimates how many hours automation would save. The estimate is a guess dressed as a number.

Vista replaces the guess. A small recorder runs on a handful of desktops for two weeks. It logs clicks, keystrokes counts, window titles and copy-paste links — redacted on the device before a byte is written — and a dependency-free engine turns that stream into steps, cases and a directly-follows graph.

The result is a map where every edge has a frequency and a wait time, every rework loop is counted, and every “we re-key this from the PDF” claim comes with a number: Acrobat → QuickBooks, 42 times, 3.1 hours a week.

Screens are one evidence source, not the only one. Phone calls, paper and meetings never reach a recorder, so employees annotate what happened off-screen and their word outranks our rules. Observed facts, inference and testimony stay separable all the way to the report.

Clicks become steps. Steps become cases. Cases become a decision.

  1. Record

    One button in a small desktop app. An always-on overlay shows the employee it is running. Private apps and title keywords mute capture entirely.

  2. Redact

    Emails, phone numbers, IBANs and card numbers are removed from titles and clipboard before storage. Typed characters are never kept, only counts.

  3. Abstract

    Ordered rules lift raw events into business activities. Consecutive events of one activity merge into a step with counts of keys, copies and pastes.

  4. Correlate

    Invoice, ticket and PO numbers are read from titles and URLs and carried across the session so every screen lands in its case.

  5. Discover

    A directly-follows graph with frequency and mean wait per edge, variants with throughput time, rework detection. Exports to CSV and XES.

  6. Rank

    Frequency, regularity, data transfer and app switching score each activity. Cross-app pastes count double. Hours are observed, not promised.

The recorder asks. The employee answers. Their word wins.

Unexplained steps and long gaps become interview questions. Answers are appended as annotations and the pipeline runs again — with human-supplied labels and case IDs never overwritten by a rule.
