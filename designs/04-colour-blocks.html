Nothing leaves the laptop that a person hasn't seen.

The recorder redacts on device, the engine has no I/O beyond files, and the only thing uploaded is a report summary plus the activity log. Screenshots and video stay put.

Redacted before written

Emails, phones, IBANs and cards are replaced in titles and clipboard text before a line hits disk. Typed characters are never stored.

Provenance on every step

observed, rule, filled, episode or human. Facts, inference and testimony never blend.

People outrank rules

An employee's annotation splits and relabels our steps. Their case IDs are never overwritten.
