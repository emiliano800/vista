# Northfield Industrial Components — Operating Procedures (excerpt)

1. **Quote-to-order.** RFQs logged in Epicor within 1 business day; quotes valid 30 days; engineering review for any non-catalog part.
2. **Credit.** Orders over credit limit or with any 90+ balance route to Controller before release. Credit hold is a hard stop in the ERP.
3. **Purchasing.** POs > $10k need President approval. Three-way match tolerance 2% / $50.
4. **Receiving.** Raw material requires cert check and lot tag; inspection_required items go to QC hold bin.
5. **Quality.** NCRs with capa_required must have a CAPA opened within 10 days. Gauges out of cal are quarantined.
6. **Engineering changes.** ECO release must update BOM the same day (bom_updated = Y). Planner verifies before next WO release.
7. **Month-end.** Inventory valuation, PPV and cycle-count adjustments posted by WD+3.
